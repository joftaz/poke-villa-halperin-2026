# House Bowl image prompts

The committed assets are square, top-down, photorealistic food photographs on a dark charcoal surface. They intentionally contain no text, branding, utensils, hands, or props.

## `green-villa.webp` — הירוקה של הווילה

> Create a square 1:1 premium food photograph for a modern poke restaurant web app. Photorealistic top-down overhead composition of one handcrafted ceramic poke bowl centered on a matte dark charcoal stone surface. The bowl contains white rice, wakame seaweed, sliced avocado, alfalfa sprouts, edamame, finely sliced green onion, golden seared tofu cubes, and a subtle soy sauce glaze. Fresh, abundant, carefully separated ingredients, natural appetizing textures, restrained editorial styling, moody soft side lighting, warm highlights, high-end restaurant menu photography. Keep the full bowl visible with generous dark negative space around it. No utensils, no hands, no props, no text, no typography, no logo, no watermark. This is the green bowl in a three-image series; keep camera angle, bowl shape, surface, lighting, crop, and color grade consistent across the series.
